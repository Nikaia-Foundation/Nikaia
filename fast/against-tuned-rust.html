Against optimised Rust

The goal
Nikaia's goal is the best code for everything the compiler can see: you write the plain program, and making it fast is the compiler's job. You leave that path only to go close to the hardware, for what the standard library does not offer your special case, or for knowledge about your input no compiler can have. Here is our program for the One Billion Row Challenge. Read it, then look at the numbers.

Nikaia / AI variant 1 / AI variant 2

AI variant 1 is an AI assistant's program from one request; AI variant 2 is the same assistant's after several rounds, each measured and improved (why two AI variants). Nikaia's plain program is ahead of AI variant 1 at 16 units, level with it at 1 unit, and behind AI variant 2. The aim is to play at the level of the best hand-optimised programs, so that nobody depends on having the one programmer who optimises every task to the end.

Counted with callgrind, release builds on both sides, the same output byte for byte; Nikaia keeps UTF-8 and overflow checked.
