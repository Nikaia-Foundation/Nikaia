# How we build Nikaia

**Strategy.** Correctness first, then self-hosting: writing the compiler in Nikaia tests the language for real and finds bugs; faster CI and the `rs2nika` converter serve those two and count only as far as they speed them. We optimise the converter to reach self-hosting faster and safer, not for its own sake.

**Formula.** The next topic is the one with the highest

    Priority = (strategic value + operational value) / total cost

over one horizon. Strategic value: self-hosting progress, compiler bugs found and fixed, fewer known open bugs, trust in the language. Operational value: tokens saved by faster CI, less manual translation, less debugging and fewer workarounds, shorter feedback loops, fewer regressions. Cost: tokens for implementation, tests and integration, added maintenance, and the self-hosting progress delayed meanwhile. Progress is counted in Rust lines replaced, not Nikaia tokens, since Nikaia code is shorter. Bugs first is the default; the score overrides it when the figures say so. The estimate per ticket are in [`docs/priorities.md`](docs/priorities.md).

**Consulting agent.** Proposals for how we work no longer come only from the owner or from outside advisers asked from experience: a consulting agent on the team reads our runs (transcripts, CI steps) and proposes measures, ranked. Each measure the architect takes is a row below, with its hope and its measurement kept apart.
