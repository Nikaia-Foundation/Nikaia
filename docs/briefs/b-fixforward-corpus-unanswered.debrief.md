1. **What cost the most.** The bootstrap loop cost the most, and part of it was my own doing. I wrote `the_types_meet_with_holes` as a `match (a, b)` over two references. That exposed a real lowering defect: the generated code had `ref x` under implicit borrowing and a `&bool` that was never copied out. Fixing it meant a second checker change, a one-time hand patch of the generated `lowered/check_arms.rs` to get past the bootstrap, and then confirming the fixpoint by md5. Second was the first gate run, which went red on NK1245 (a whole `Unknown` arm was being treated as a hole) and needed another build-and-test round.

2. **What I looked up that the brief could have handed me.** I had to find where arms meet (`check_core_flow_meet` and the order it tries: text, null, disagree) and how `copied_bindings` and `lent_bindings` decide between `let x = *x;` and `ref x`. I also checked that NK1245 (`arms_that_disagree`) is the refusal that depends on an arm staying `Unknown`. The brief named the counter and the suspect lines well. That made the find step cheap: one temporary `eprintln!` showed the two prover calls.

3. **Checks run.** I ran `sequences::` a few times, a probe of the defect against the unfixed compiler, the guard set (37), `tests/compiler` at both settings (369), clippy and `pre-push-check.sh`. None was really wasted; the red first gate found a real regression. Two things went poorly:
   - The push was rejected, and the brief forbids rebasing. So a fix-forward commit is sitting unpushed and `develop` stays red until someone rebases it.
   - Because the push failed, `commit.nika` ran before the `Disk freed:` line was filled in. I put the `DISK` placeholder there myself, so the order was my mistake.

4. **One change that would have saved the most.** Let fix-forward briefs `git pull --rebase origin develop` and push again when the push is rejected. CLAUDE.md's trunk rules already say to do that, and they contradict builder.md's "no rebase". A fix that isn't pushed fixes nothing. A smaller second change: one line in builder-facts.md saying that a new checker helper should avoid any form the checker change itself touches, to avoid the bootstrap problem.
